# The Way of St. Francis

*Journeying to Heaven with Christ the Way and Paradigm and Christ as Brother and Friend*

**Play:** https://lukasjesso.github.io/way-of-st-francis/

A browser game for THEO 20262, *The Person of Christ* (University of Notre Dame, Fall 2026). It pairs Session 15, "Way and Paradigm" (Pelikan, *Jesus Through the Centuries*, ch. 11; 1 John 3), with Session 16, "Friend and Brother" (John 15; Hebrews 2; *Christ and Abba Menas*; *Buddy Christ*).

You walk the life of Francis of Assisi beside Christ, choosing between three doors at each station. The game was made with the help of generative AI.

Controls: ← → to walk, ↑ to go through a door (or click a door), H to call on Christ, M to mute.
